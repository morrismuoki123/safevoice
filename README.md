SafeVoice aims to encourage reporting, improve access to support services, increase awareness of GBV and help relevant support organisations understand reported incidents.

Confidentiality, secure handling of reports, informed consent and protection of survivors' identities are essential to its implementation. The platform should not replace emergency services or professional counselling.
